A timeline of movie genres

Genre history does not have one exact starting date. These milestones show important periods and developments, rather than the first time a genre ever appeared.

Late 1800s–1900s

01. Western

Frontiers & choices

Western films often take place in the American West, especially during the frontier period. They commonly explore law and order, survival, justice, courage, and conflict between individuals or communities. Wide landscapes, horses, small frontier towns, and showdowns are common features. The genre changed over time, with later Westerns often questioning the simple idea of heroes and villains.

Frontier lifeJusticeSurvival

Influences & key figures

Frontier stories, American history, and popular adventure stories influenced the genre. Directors such as John Ford helped shape the classic Western, while Sergio Leone brought a distinctive style to the Spaghetti Western.

Movies to explore

The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (1966)Unforgiven (1992)Tombstone (1993)True Grit (2010)Once Upon a Time in the West (1968)
1890s–present

02. Comedy

Humour & everyday life

Comedy aims to entertain and make audiences laugh. It uses situations such as misunderstandings, surprising events, funny characters, wordplay, and physical humour. Silent-film performers used movement and facial expressions to tell jokes before spoken dialogue became common. Today, comedy can mix with romance, action, drama, and other genres.

Physical humourSatireSituational comedy

Influences & key figures

Stage comedy and silent cinema helped shape the genre. Charlie Chaplin became known for physical comedy, while filmmakers such as Billy Wilder helped develop comedy with sharp dialogue and social observations.

Movies to explore

Back to the Future (1985)Groundhog Day (1993)Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975)Superbad (2007)Some Like It Hot (1959)
1890s–present

03. Horror

Fear & suspense

Horror is designed to create fear, tension, or unease. Its stories may involve supernatural events, monsters, dangerous situations, or fears found in everyday life. Low lighting, unsettling sounds, suspense, and unexpected events are common techniques. Horror changes with the worries of its audience and often reflects the concerns of its time.

SuspenseMonstersThe unknown

Influences & key figures

Gothic literature, folklore, and early special effects influenced horror cinema. Directors such as Alfred Hitchcock shaped suspense, while George A. Romero influenced modern zombie films.

Movies to explore

The Thing (1982)The Shining (1980)Alien (1979)Hereditary (2018)Get Out (2017)
1900s–present

04. Action

Movement & danger

Action films focus on physical challenges, fast movement, danger, and exciting set pieces. They often include chases, fights, rescues, escapes, or missions. Stunts, editing, sound, and visual effects help create energy and tension. Modern action films often combine action with science fiction, comedy, adventure, or superhero stories.

StuntsChasesMissions

Influences & key figures

Adventure serials, martial arts cinema, stunt performance, and advances in editing helped shape action films. Bruce Lee influenced martial arts action, while directors such as George Miller became known for energetic action sequences.

Movies to explore

Die Hard (1988)Terminator 2: Judgment Day (1991)Mad Max: Fury Road (2015)The Dark Knight (2008)John Wick (2014)
1900s–present

05. Science Fiction

Ideas & the future

Science fiction explores imagined worlds, future technologies, space travel, robots, time travel, or possible scientific discoveries. It often asks how science and technology could change people and society. Its settings and visual effects help audiences imagine things that do not exist yet. The genre can be exciting and adventurous, but it can also explore serious questions about humanity and the future.

TechnologySpaceFuture societies

Influences & key figures

Scientific discoveries, space exploration, and science-fiction literature influenced the genre. Georges Méliès experimented with imaginative film effects, while directors such as Stanley Kubrick and George Lucas helped shape influential science-fiction films.

Movies to explore

Interstellar (2014)The Matrix (1999)Blade Runner 2049 (2017)Inception (2010)2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)

What shapes a genre?

More than a movie category

Genres are not fixed rules. They develop as filmmakers experiment and audiences respond to new stories, styles, and ideas.

🌍

History & society

Events, cultural changes, and the concerns of different generations can affect the stories filmmakers choose to tell.

🎥

People & creativity

Directors, writers, actors, producers, composers, and designers bring new styles and ideas that other films may build on.

⚙️

Technology

Sound, colour, editing, computer-generated imagery, and new cameras have changed how filmmakers create movie worlds.

Quick guide

How to recognise each genre

Use these common features as clues. A film can belong to more than one genre, so no single feature guarantees its category.

Action

Fast-paced events, physical challenges, stunts, chases, and high-risk missions.

Comedy

Humorous situations, funny characters, misunderstandings, and jokes.

Horror

Fear, suspense, unsettling events, threats, and the unknown.

Science fiction

Imagined science, future technology, space, robots, or alternate worlds.

Western

Frontier settings, conflicts over justice, survival, and life in the Old West.

Genres can mix

A film may combine categories, such as science-fiction action or horror comedy.

About this project

Made to explore film history

This website was created as a learning project to research and categorise five movie genres, explore their background and influences, identify important film figures, and connect each genre with well-known movie examples. The timeline and sections are designed to make the information clear and easy to explore.
